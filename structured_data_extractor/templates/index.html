Under the hood

How it works

01

Parse

Your file is read server-side using pdfplumber, python-docx or plain UTF-8. Raw text is extracted preserving layout cues.

02

Extract

Section headings are detected via a 50+ alias dictionary. Contact details, skills, experience and education are parsed with heuristics.

03

Score & Validate

A completeness score is computed across 6 weighted dimensions and validated against a Pydantic v2 schema.

04

Deliver

Browse results page-by-page, download JSON, print a report or compare two candidates. No files are ever stored.
